How much does an AI 3D modeler cost?

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Most hosted AI 3D modelers start at around $12 to $20 a month. A credit buys a different amount of work at every vendor, so compare the cost of a finished, textured model, not the credit count. Prices below are in US dollars before tax. They come from each vendor's own pricing page unless marked reported, which means the official page could not be read and the figure comes from a dated November 2026 guide. Promotions and regional prices change often, so check the checkout price before you buy.

Approximate cost per textured model

  • Tripo API: $0.20 (text-to-3D with standard texture) or $0.30 (image-to-3D), from Tripo's published API price list.
  • Sloyd API: about $0.33 for a textured text-to-3D model (25 credits at 75 credits per dollar), from Sloyd's API rate card.
  • Hunyuan3D API: roughly $0.38 to $0.70, depending on options (reported).
  • Rodin Creator: roughly $0.40 to $0.50 at standard settings, based on about 60 models per plan.
  • Meshy Pro: up to 100 assets per $20, so at least $0.20 per asset. Independent guides estimate about $0.60 for a textured Meshy 6 image-to-3D model.
  • Sloyd Plus: core text- and image-to-3D generation in the web app is unlimited for a flat fee.

Hidden costs to watch

  • Annual-billing prices shown as monthly (Tripo, Rodin, Sloyd, Spline).
  • First-month or first-year promotions that end (Meshy, Hi3D).
  • Surcharges for HD textures, 8K maps, quads, multi-view input and higher geometry quality.
  • Subscription credits that reset each billing cycle (Spline states credits don't roll over).
  • API credits billed separately from app subscriptions (Sloyd).
  • Free tiers that don't allow commercial use or downloads.
